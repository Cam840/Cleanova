Our Story

Built from a simple frustration.

Cleanova was founded after years of watching property managers, business owners, and homeowners settle for cleaning services that didn't return calls, didn't follow through, and didn't take the work seriously.

We built this company to be the opposite — a facility services group with the systems and accountability of a corporate operation, the polish of a luxury brand, and the trust of a partner who actually shows up.

From contracted office janitorial to discreet luxury home care, every Cleanova engagement runs on the same foundation: communicate clearly, execute consistently, present professionally.
