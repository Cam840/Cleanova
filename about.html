Our Story

Built from a simple frustration.

Cleanova was founded after years of watching property managers, business owners, and homeowners settle for cleaning services that didn't return calls, didn't follow through, and didn't take the work seriously.

We started this company to be the opposite of all that — a facility services group with the systems and accountability of a corporate operation, the polish of a luxury brand, and the trust of a partner who actually shows up.

From contracted office janitorial to discreet luxury home maintenance, every Cleanova engagement runs on the same foundation: communicate clearly, execute consistently, present professionally.

What Makes Us Different

Built like a corporate operation.
Run like a partner.

Most local cleaning services are built around a single owner with a van. Cleanova was designed from day one as a facility services group — with structured systems, trained crews, account management, and quality assurance built in.

  • Same-day quotes — most submissions answered the same business day, with scope, schedule, and pricing.
  • Trained, vetted crews — every team member is background-checked, uniformed, and trained on Cleanova protocols.
  • Commercial-grade equipment — auto-scrubbers, HEPA extraction, low-moisture systems, and modern robotic floor equipment.
  • Documented QA — checklists, audits, and review cycles built into every recurring contract.
  • Flexible scheduling — after-hours, weekends, overnight. We work around your operation, not the other way around.
  • Satisfaction guarantee — if something is missed or below standard, we return and re-do it. No invoicing games.
