Post-Construction System

Three tiers to move-in ready.

New construction and renovation cleaning isn't one visit — it's a structured process. Our three-phase system takes your property from job site to showcase.

01
Initial Clean

Rough Phase

  • Debris and construction waste removal
  • Concrete, drywall, and sawdust sweep-down
  • Window frame and door jamb wipedown
  • Rough floor prep for finishing work
  • Trash haul-out and dumpster coordination
02
Detailed Clean

Detail Phase

  • Interior glass, mirrors, and shower doors
  • Cabinet interior and exterior wipedown
  • Fixture and hardware polishing
  • Paint overspray and sticker residue removal
  • HVAC vent and return air cleaning
03
Final Presentation

Final Phase

  • Full floor polish, buff, and protect
  • Touch-up cleaning on all surfaces
  • Final glass polish and streak inspection
  • Exterior sweep and entrance detail
  • Walk-through with client or GC
