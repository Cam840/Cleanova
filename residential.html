Post-Construction

The 3-Tier Construction Clean System.

New builds, renovations, and major work leave a specific trail. Our three-tier protocol takes a property from raw construction to move-in presentation — without the gaps most companies miss.

01
Initial Clean

Debris & Rough Cleanup

The first pass clears the heavy lifting — construction debris, dust, and surface accumulation that has to come out before refinement can begin.

  • Construction debris removal
  • Heavy dust clearing
  • Rough surface wipe-down
  • Floor preparation
  • Window and frame de-stickering
02
Detailed Clean

Surface & Fixture Detailing

The second pass goes into every fixture, finish, and surface — addressing the residue and overspray that initial cleaning leaves behind.

  • Cabinet interior & exterior cleaning
  • Fixture, trim, and hardware detail
  • Window wipe-downs (both sides)
  • Floor detailing & polishing prep
  • Vent, light, and ceiling fan care
03
Final Presentation

Move-In Ready Polish

The final pass is finish work — the level of detail that separates "clean" from "showroom." This is the tier that gets the listing photos shot.

  • Final polish on glass & stainless
  • Showroom-quality detailing
  • Luxury finishing touches
  • Walk-through inspection
  • Move-in ready presentation
